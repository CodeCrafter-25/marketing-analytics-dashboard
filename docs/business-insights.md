# Business Insights and Recommendations

## Executive Summary

The analysis covers 120 marketing campaigns across five advertising channels from January 2023 to December 2025.

The campaigns generated:

- **$4.52M** in advertising spend
- **$21.56M** in attributed revenue
- **16,691** purchases
- **4.77x** overall ROAS
- **$270.99** average cost per purchase

Affiliate and Paid Search were the strongest-performing channels, while Video and Display showed the greatest opportunities for cost-efficiency improvement.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Affiliate generated the highest return

Affiliate produced **$5.61M in attributed revenue** from **$857.84K in spend**, resulting in the highest channel ROAS of **6.54x**.

It also had the lowest cost per purchase at **$198.02**, making it the most cost-efficient channel.

### 2. Paid Search combined strong engagement with high profitability

Paid Search generated **$5.44M in revenue** and achieved a **6.06x ROAS**.

It recorded the highest CTR at **3.49%** and a relatively low cost per purchase of **$213.39**, indicating strong advertising relevance and purchase efficiency.

### 3. Paid Social delivered average portfolio performance

Paid Social had the highest advertising spend at **$984.73K** and generated **$4.69M in revenue**.

Its ROAS of **4.76x** was approximately equal to the overall portfolio ROAS of **4.77x**, suggesting stable but not leading performance.

### 4. Display showed lower advertising efficiency

Display generated **$3.73M in revenue** from **$955.30K in spend**, resulting in a **3.91x ROAS**.

Its cost per purchase was **$332.74**, considerably higher than Affiliate and Paid Search. Display also had the lowest CTR at **0.60%**.

### 5. Video was the weakest-performing channel

Video generated **$2.09M in revenue** from **$828.09K in spend**.

It had the lowest ROAS at **2.52x** and the highest cost per purchase at **$505.55**, making it the primary channel requiring optimization.

### 6. Post-lead conversion performance was consistent across channels

Lead-to-conversion rates ranged from approximately **14.94% to 15.35%** across all five channels.

Revenue per purchase was also relatively consistent. This suggests that the major differences in channel performance were driven primarily by advertising and acquisition costs rather than major differences after lead generation.

## Recommendations

### 1. Prioritize Affiliate and Paid Search

Consider allocating additional incremental budget to Affiliate and Paid Search while monitoring whether their ROAS remains stable as spending increases.

### 2. Optimize Video before increasing its budget

Review Video targeting, creative formats, placements, and campaign objectives. Further investment should depend on measurable improvements in cost per purchase and ROAS.

### 3. Improve Display engagement

Test new creatives, audience segments, placements, and landing-page alignment to improve Display CTR and reduce acquisition costs.

### 4. Analyze Paid Social at the campaign level

Paid Social performance is close to the portfolio average, but its high total spend creates an opportunity to identify and scale its strongest individual campaigns.

### 5. Use controlled budget reallocation

Budget changes should be tested gradually. Historical ROAS does not guarantee that the same performance will continue after a significant increase in spending.

## Analytical Limitations

- The analysis shows historical associations and does not establish causal impact.
- Revenue is based on campaign attribution available in the source data.
- The dataset does not include customer lifetime value or long-term repeat-purchase behavior.
- Channel-level results may contain significant differences between individual campaigns and objectives.
