# Business Questions

## Business Context

The marketing team manages campaigns across five channels:

- Affiliate
- Display
- Paid Search
- Paid Social
- Video

The campaigns support three different objectives:

- Awareness
- Lead generation
- Acquisition

The primary analytical goal is to evaluate marketing performance and identify opportunities to improve budget allocation.

## Primary Business Decision

How should the marketing budget be allocated across channels and campaigns to maximize purchases and attributed revenue while controlling acquisition costs?

## Business Questions

### 1. Overall Performance

What are the total planned budget, actual spend, impressions, clicks, leads, purchases, attributed revenue, and ROAS?

### 2. Budget Utilization

How much of the planned marketing budget was used?

Which channels and campaigns were significantly under or over their planned budgets?

### 3. Channel Performance

Which marketing channels generate the strongest results based on:

- Click-Through Rate
- Cost per Click
- Lead volume
- Cost per Lead
- Purchase volume
- Cost per Purchase
- Attributed revenue
- ROAS

### 4. Campaign Performance

Which individual campaigns generate the highest revenue and ROAS?

Which campaigns have high spending but relatively weak purchase or revenue results?

### 5. Marketing Funnel

Where are the largest performance differences in the marketing funnel?

The available funnel is:

Impressions → Clicks → Leads → Conversion

Conversions are divided into:

- Trial
- Purchase

### 6. Campaign Objective Performance

How do awareness, lead-generation, and acquisition campaigns differ in reach, engagement, lead generation, purchases, revenue, and ROAS?

Campaigns with different objectives should not be evaluated using only one common efficiency metric.

### 7. Performance Over Time

How do spend, leads, purchases, revenue, and ROAS change by month?

Are there periods of improving or declining marketing efficiency?

### 8. Budget Reallocation

Which channels or campaigns are the strongest candidates for increased investment?

Which campaigns may require budget reduction, optimization, or additional investigation?

## Dashboard Audience

The dashboard is designed for:

- Marketing managers
- Performance marketing specialists
- Business and data analysts

## Planned Dashboard Filters

- Date
- Channel
- Campaign
- Campaign objective
- Conversion type

## Analysis Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and represents a fictional business.
- Attributed revenue does not prove that marketing activity caused the conversion.
- The dataset does not contain customer segments.
- Each converted lead has only one conversion record.
- Trial-to-purchase progression cannot be tracked.
- Customer lifetime value and repeat purchases cannot be calculated.
- Cost per Purchase should not be presented as full Customer Acquisition Cost because the dataset does not contain all customer-related business costs.
